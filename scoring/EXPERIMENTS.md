# Experiment Overview

## synthetic_power_comparison

Tests whether a certification pipeline has enough power to certify a reliable model when the auxiliary judge is informative and many judge labels are available.

## oracle_gap

Measures the loss caused by having to estimate judge reliability from a small calibration set instead of knowing the judge reliability exactly.

## synthetic_type2_curve

Checks how the chance of failing to certify changes as the model's true failure rate moves from very safe to above the certification threshold.

## synthetic_type1_control

Checks whether the certification procedure avoids falsely certifying an unsafe borderline model at the required significance level.
